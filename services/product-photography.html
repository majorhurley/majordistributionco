Product photography

Product and brand photos that make people want the thing, shot on location or on a clean background and edited from RAW.

Start a projectSee the work

What you get

A shoot planned around where the photos will live: your Instagram grid, your online store, ads, menus or print. We shoot on a Sony body, edit every frame from RAW, and deliver a post-ready set in the sizes you need, from square feed posts to vertical stories to wide web banners.

On location

Some products sell the place they come from. For Mad Duck Craft Brewing we took the cans outdoors, across hills, a river and a gorge, and edited the shoot into a rollout set the brewery could post over weeks.

Studio and clean background

When the product needs to stand on its own, we shoot it clean. For PeacePal we ran a studio shoot against a flag backdrop and a set of clean-background product shots, then built a wordmark and product graphic on top of the photography.

People and portraits

Brands are people too. We shot staff headshots for the barbers at Golden Vision Barbershop, made to match across the team so the shop could use them on social and its booking pages.

Where we work

We're based in La Verne and shoot across Pomona, Claremont, San Dimas, the San Gabriel Valley, Los Angeles and Orange County.

FAQ

Questions

Can you shoot at our business?

Yes. Most shoots happen at your location or somewhere that fits the product. We bring what we need for a clean background when the product calls for one.

Do we get edited photos or raw files?

Edited photos, exported in the sizes you'll actually use. Every frame is edited from RAW.

Can the same shoot cover photos and video?

Yes, and it usually should. One visit can produce product stills, Reels and ad footage, which keeps your feed consistent and saves a second shoot.
