Social media management

We run your Instagram and TikTok: original content every week, written captions and a calendar, so your feed never goes quiet.

Start a projectSee the work

What you get

A monthly plan that keeps your accounts active with original content, not reposts. That means shooting at your business, editing Reels, static posts and carousels, writing captions and hashtags for every piece, and scheduling it all on a weekly posting calendar.

Original content every week

For Cafe Wang, a Chinese fusion restaurant in La Verne, the plan was two original pieces a week across Reels, static posts and carousels, opened with a promo film shot in the restaurant. Every piece came with its caption, hashtags and a slot on the calendar.

Account management and reporting

For Mad Duck Craft Brewing we managed the @madduckcraft Instagram, an account of about 9,300 followers, and built performance reporting so the brewery could see what was working. Around it we made a canning-line commercial with short cutdowns for TikTok and Reels, a 12-post TikTok and Reels plan with hooks, captions and shot notes, and post and story designs.

Built from your own footage

Because we shoot the content ourselves, every post looks like your business. It's your food, your product, your staff. One shoot day feeds weeks of posts.

Where we work

We're based in La Verne and work with businesses across Pomona, Claremont, San Dimas, the San Gabriel Valley, Los Angeles and Orange County.
