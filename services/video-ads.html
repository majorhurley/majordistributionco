Video ads

Vertical video ads for Meta, Instagram Reels and TikTok, shot at your business and cut to stop the scroll.

Start a projectSee the work

What you get

We come to your shop, restaurant or brewery and shoot the real thing: the work, the product, the people who do it. Then we cut it into short vertical ads with captions held clear of the Reels and TikTok buttons, licensed music, and an offer graphic when the ad needs one. You get finished files ready to upload to Meta Ads Manager or post organically.

Shot where you work

Stock footage doesn't sell a haircut or a pint. For Golden Vision Barbershop we filmed on the floor at both locations, Santa Ana and Tustin: real cuts, before-and-after transformations, and barbers talking straight to camera. For Mad Duck Craft Brewing we filmed the canning line. For Cafe Wang in La Verne we shot a 4K promo film in the restaurant itself: the wok, the plates, the room and the storefront.

Cut for the feed

People decide in the first second whether to keep watching, so every ad opens on the strongest moment. Ads run roughly 15 to 30 seconds, with on-screen captions for people watching muted. When the location audio is noisy we clean the dialogue off the background music with stem separation, then score the cut with a licensed track.

Offers that convert

An ad without a reason to act is just a video. We design first-time-customer offer graphics into the edit. Golden Vision ran a $35 first cut in Santa Ana and $45 in Tustin, so each location's ad carried its own price.

Where we work

We're based in La Verne and shoot across Pomona, Claremont, San Dimas, the San Gabriel Valley, Los Angeles and Orange County.

FAQ

Questions

Do you run the ads too, or only make them?

We make the creative and hand over finished files sized for each placement. If you want help putting them in front of the right people, ask about campaign setup when you get in touch.

Which platforms are the ads made for?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram, including Reels and Stories) and TikTok. We deliver vertical by default, and 16:9 versions when a placement needs them.

Do you come to our location?

Yes. Every shoot happens at your business, anywhere from La Verne and the San Gabriel Valley to Los Angeles and Orange County.

Is the music licensed?

Yes. We score ads with licensed tracks, so they can run as paid ads without copyright strikes.
