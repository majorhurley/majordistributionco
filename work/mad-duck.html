Mad Duck Craft Brewing

Instagram management, product photography, a canning-day commercial and the social cuts around it.

The job

Mad Duck Craft Brewing needed a steady stream of content that looked like the brewery: the beer, the cans and the places people drink them. From May to September 2026 we managed the @madduckcraft Instagram, shot the product on location, filmed the canning line, and planned and cut the short-form video around it.

What we did

  • Managed the @madduckcraft Instagram (9.3K followers) and built its performance reporting
  • Location product shoot across hills, river and gorge, edited into a post-ready rollout set
  • Canning-line commercial plus short cutdowns for TikTok and Reels
  • A 12-post TikTok and Reels plan with hooks, captions and shot notes
  • Pop ads in vertical and 16:9, and post and story designs built as code
