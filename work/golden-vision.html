Golden Vision Barbershop

A run of Meta Reels ads for two barbershop locations.

The job

Golden Vision Barbershop has two locations, in Santa Ana and Tustin, and needed ads that would bring first-time clients through the door at each one. We shot on the floor at both shops and cut a run of vertical Meta Reels ads around real cuts, transformations and the barbers themselves, each ad carrying its location's first-visit offer.

What we did

  • Shot on the floor at both shops: cuts, transformations, barbers talking to camera
  • Cut each ad to a 17–28 second vertical with captions held clear of the Reels UI
  • Built first-time-client offer graphics ($35 Santa Ana, $45 Tustin)
  • Cleaned dialogue off the shop's background music with stem separation, then scored with licensed tracks
