Cafe Wang

Instagram content and management for a Chinese fusion restaurant, opened with a promo film.

The job

Cafe Wang is a Chinese fusion restaurant in La Verne and Major Distribution's first signed client. The job was to keep its Instagram active with original content every week. We opened with a 4K promo film shot in the restaurant, then ran a monthly plan of Reels, static posts and carousels, each with its own caption, hashtags and slot on the calendar.

What we did

  • Major Distribution's first signed client
  • Monthly Instagram engagement: two original pieces a week across Reels, static posts and carousels
  • Captions, hashtags and a weekly posting calendar for every piece
  • 4K promo film shot in the restaurant: the wok, the plates, the room, the storefront
  • Logo cleanup for print and screen
